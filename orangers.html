O'Rangers

Bitcoin is orange. $ORNG is the coin, and it trades on BONS. O'Rangers are the people: one picture each, from the head to the shoulders.

A picture on Bitcoin is one inscription. The collection note for that is CRC-21. Look still reads coins. It does not list these pictures.

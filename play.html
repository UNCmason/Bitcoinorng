How to play

THE BASICS
How do I play?

Swipe your finger (or hold and drag your mouse) across oranges to slice them. Slice enough to clear a level, and do not let them fall off the bottom.

How many lives do I have?

You start with 5 hearts (top right). At the start of each new world you win back one lost heart. 5 is the maximum.

When do I lose a life?

When a regular orange, a mini orange or the boss orange falls off the bottom, or when you slice a FUD bomb or fake gold. Anything else falling past costs nothing.

Does it work on desktop?

Yes. Hold the left mouse button and drag across the oranges.

How do I pause?

Tap ❚❚ in the bottom corner. The game also pauses by itself if you switch apps or tabs.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap 🔊 in the bottom corner. Your choice is remembered. If a phone plays nothing, tap the screen once and check that silent mode is off.

ORANGES AND POWER-UPS
What are the different oranges?

Regular oranges are worth 1 Slice. Mini oranges (from level 12) are small and fast, worth 2. Golden ₿ oranges are worth 21.

What does the golden ₿ orange do?

It gives 21 Slices and starts Bitcoin Time: 3 seconds of slow motion with a golden trail.

What is the ⚡ Lightning orange?

It appears from World 2. Slice it and lightning zaps everything on screen. Oranges are sliced for points and hazards are wiped out for a couple of Slices each.

What is 🔒 Cold Storage?

It appears from World 6. Slice it to get a shield (🛡 next to your hearts) that blocks the next time you would lose a life.

What is the BONS orange?

It appears from World 7 and is named after bonsonbtc.fun, the launchpad $ORNG launched on. Slice it for BONS Boost: 6 seconds of triple Slices while the oranges launch higher and float longer.

THINGS TO AVOID
What is the FUD bomb?

A dark bomb that appears from level 5. Slicing it costs you a life.

What is FEAR?

A purple orange from World 3. Slicing it rolls in a fog for 5 seconds so you only see around your finger, and it resets your streak. It does not cost a life.

What is FIAT?

A green orange from World 4. Slicing it inflates away 10% of your Slices. It does not cost a life.

How do I tell real gold from fake gold?

Real gold shows a ₿. Fake gold (from World 5) shows a $ and looks slightly greener. Slicing the fake costs a life.

Can I just let hazards fall?

Yes. Letting any hazard fall past costs nothing, so when in doubt, do not slice it.

SCORING
What are Slices?

Your score. Slice oranges to stack Slices and climb the weekly board. They are not Bitcoin and have no cash value on their own.

What is a combo?

Slice 3 or more in one swipe and every extra cut earns +1 Slice.

What is the streak multiplier?

Slice 10 in a row without a miss for ×2, 20 for ×3, 30 for ×4 and 40 for ×5. It resets when you lose a life or hit FEAR.

What is ORNG Frenzy?

Every 25 slices you get 6 seconds of non-stop oranges, no bombs and double Slices. Oranges you miss during a frenzy cost nothing, and it starts gentle in the early levels. It does not happen on boss levels.

What are Halving levels?

Level 5 of every world. You get half as many oranges, but every one is worth double.

What are the Slice milestones?

A banner pops at 21, 210, 2,100, 21,000 and 210,000 Slices.

LEVELS AND PROGRESS
How many levels are there?

210 levels in 21 worlds of 10 levels each. Every world has a Bitcoin theme and ends with a boss.

How do I clear a level?

Slice a set number of oranges. It starts at 5 and rises to 25 later in the game. On boss levels you clear it by defeating the boss.

How do I beat a boss?

Swipe through the big orange again and again until its health bar is empty. It falls slowly. If it drops off the bottom you lose a life and it returns with the same health.

What happens after level 210?

Endless mode unlocks. The worlds keep coming as Hyperbitcoinization, with no finish line.

What is a checkpoint?

After a game over you can continue from the first level of the world you reached. You can also restart from Level 1 whenever you like.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your own device and browser. There is no account. Clearing your browser data, or switching browser or device, starts you fresh.

What are the ranks?

Pulp Rookie, Zest Hunter (Level 20), Juice Master (50), Orange Whale (100), Satoshi's Chef (150) and Bitcoin Orange (clear all 210 levels).

What is the daily streak?

The 🔥 count on the menu shows how many days in a row you have played.
